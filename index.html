Step 1 — Supplier KPI dataset. Aggregated from 100 SKUs across 5 suppliers. TOPSIS evaluates each supplier's Euclidean distance from the ideal best and ideal worst across all criteria simultaneously. Green = best tier, amber = mid, red = weakest.

Step 2 — Criterion weights. Assign a relative importance (1–10) to each KPI. Weights are automatically normalised to sum to 1.0. Higher = more important to your procurement strategy.

Step 3 — TOPSIS computation trail. The normalised weighted matrix is built from vector-normalised KPI values multiplied by your weights. PIS = best value per criterion, NIS = worst. Closeness Ci = D² / (D¹ + D²) — higher is better.
